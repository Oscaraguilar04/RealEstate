About

A practice built around attention, not volume.

Esme works with a small number of clients at a time. The work is straightforward: understand the brief, protect what matters, and move with intention. She is known for quiet follow-through and for making complex decisions feel manageable.

The value is simple. You get a clear point of contact, honest counsel, and a process that respects your time.
